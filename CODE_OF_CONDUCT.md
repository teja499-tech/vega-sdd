# Code of conduct

Vega SDD welcomes people with different backgrounds, identities, abilities, experience levels, and points of view. Participate with empathy, technical honesty, and respect.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Give actionable feedback and accept corrections gracefully.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and project boundaries.
- Make space for newcomers and communicate accessibly.
- Disclose conflicts of interest that could affect a review or decision.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, spam, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, temporarily suspend access, or permanently ban a participant when necessary to protect the community. Responses will consider impact, context, recurrence, and willingness to repair harm.

Report an incident privately through the repository's **Report a vulnerability** form, using “Code of Conduct” in the title and omitting unrelated sensitive information. If that form is unavailable, open a public issue asking for a private maintainer contact without describing the incident. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed when safety or law requires action.

This policy applies to repository activity, project communication, events, and public representation of Vega SDD. It is informed by established open-source community standards, including the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), while using project-specific language and enforcement guidance.
